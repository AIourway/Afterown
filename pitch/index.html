PROBLEM

Resale starts too late.

Most marketplaces only see an asset after its owner has already decided to sell.

01 — Decision

The owner decides an asset is surplus.

02 — Listing

The asset is photographed, described and listed.

03 — Demand

Only then does the market reveal buyer interest.

INSIGHT

What if demand appeared before the sale decision?

AfterOwn is designed to discover potential liquidity before an asset enters the resale market.

Predict

Identify owner-approved assets that may become surplus within a future period.

Match

Connect probable future supply with standing requirements from qualified buyers.

Signal

Present conditional buyer interest before conventional listing.

EXAMPLE

100 laptops. Six months early.

A simple enterprise technology validation scenario.

1

Enterprise refresh data

The owner indicates a likely replacement window.

2

Future supply signal

AfterOwn identifies approximately 100 devices that may become available.

3

Buyer requirement

A professional refurbisher wants that model and specification.

4

Conditional demand

The owner discovers potential demand before deciding whether to sell.

PRODUCT

A private pre-market, not another classifieds site.

The owner stays in control while buyers gain earlier visibility into potential supply.

Asset owners

  • Register assets or asset groups
  • Set privacy and timing rules
  • Receive conditional market signals
  • Choose whether to transact

AfterOwn

  • Estimate future availability
  • Structure asset attributes
  • Match buyer requirements
  • Measure price and interest signals

Qualified buyers

  • Define standing demand
  • Specify model, condition and timing
  • Express conditional interest
  • Prepare for future inventory
